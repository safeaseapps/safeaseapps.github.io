Safety & help

Safease by Safease Apps

Safease is a private, offline vault. Files are encrypted on the user's own phone and are never uploaded. There is no cloud, no public links and no way to share content with other Safease users. Nobody — including the developer — can see, scan, remove or recover what is inside a vault.

If your intimate images are being shared

  • Taken when you were 18 or older: StopNCII.org helps stop them spreading on participating platforms.
  • Taken when you were under 18: Take It Down (NCMEC) helps get them removed.

With both services the image never leaves your phone — only a digital fingerprint is shared.

Report child sexual abuse material

  • Any country: find your national hotline at INHOPE, the worldwide network of reporting hotlines.
  • NCMEC CyberTipline: report.cybertip.org.

Never allowed in Safease

Every user must agree to these rules before creating a vault. Breaking them ends the right to use Safease (see the Terms of Use, section 3):

  • Child sexual abuse material — any sexual or sexualised content involving anyone under 18.
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery — intimate or sexual photos or videos of anyone who did not agree to them being taken, kept or shared.
  • Any other content that is illegal where the user lives, or that they have no right to possess.

Contact the developer

To report misuse of Safease, or for law-enforcement requests: indusbyte.app@gmail.com.

Please never send us images or videos. Safease has no servers, no user accounts and no copy of anyone's files, so we cannot view, remove or trace content (see the Privacy Policy). Law enforcement can act — please report to them first.
