Privacy Policy

Safease (Android app com.indusbyte.safease)
Effective date: 31 October 2026

Safease is published on Google Play by Safease Apps, the developer name of Abdul Rehman, an individual developer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone and what the advertising and payment services built into it collect.

Safease Apps was called IndusByte until October 2026. The app's package name (com.indusbyte.safease) and the contact email still use the old name; they belong to the same developer.

In short
  • Everything you put in the vault is encrypted on your phone. I never receive your files, PIN, recovery key, contacts, notes or file names.
  • The editing and AI tools (Safease Studio, Safease Cut, captions, face and background tools) run on your phone. Your photos, videos and voice are never uploaded.
  • The app has no account, no sign-in and no servers of mine. I run no analytics of my own.
  • Crash reports are optional and off until you turn them on. They are anonymous and never contain your files, file names, PIN or recovery key.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data, as described below. You are asked for consent where the law requires it.
  • Premium (no ads) is bought through Google Play. I never see your payment details.

1. Your vault content stays on your phone

Photos, videos, music, documents, notes, contacts and any other items you add are encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device before they are stored. The keys that protect them are derived from your PIN and your recovery key and are protected by your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore). Names of files and folders are encrypted too.

This content is never uploaded to me or to any server of mine. I cannot see it, recover it or reset your PIN. If you lose both your PIN and your recovery key, nobody — including me — can open the vault.

Backups

If you turn on Backup, the app copies your vault, still encrypted, to a folder you choose. If that folder is synced by another service (for example a cloud drive app), the encrypted copies are handled by that service under its own privacy policy. Without your PIN or recovery key the copies cannot be read.

When files leave the vault

Some actions need a normal, unencrypted copy of a file: sharing it to another app, opening it in another app, or exporting it to your phone's storage. The app makes that copy only when you ask for it. Once a copy is in another app or in your phone's storage, it is no longer protected by Safease. Temporary copies the app itself makes are kept in its private storage and deleted afterwards.

Where you stopped watching or reading

To let you continue where you left off, the app remembers how far you got in each video and which page of a PDF you were reading. This is stored only inside the vault on your phone, holds no file names, and is never sent anywhere. You can turn it off or clear it in Settings › Behaviour.

Files that stay on your phone (Player, Music, PDF and Docs)

Safease Player, Safease Music, Safease PDF and Safease Docs open videos, songs, PDFs and documents that are already on your phone or SD card, without adding them to the vault. They can read only the files you pick, the folders you choose in Android's own folder picker (for example Download or Documents), and, for Music, the audio on the device if you allow it. You can remove a folder at any time in the app, which gives the access back to Android. The app does not ask for permission to read all your files.

To show lists quickly, the app keeps the names of these files and small preview pictures (for example the first page of a document) in its private storage on your phone. You can clear the previews in the app's settings. None of this is ever sent anywhere. Files you create or convert (for example a new PDF, or a PDF turned into a Word document) are saved on your phone where you choose. A file is copied into the vault, or deleted from your phone, only when you choose to.

Editing and AI tools on your phone (Safease Studio and Safease Cut)

The photo editor, Safease Studio and the Safease Cut video editor work on your phone. This includes the AI tools: auto captions (speech recognition), finding and hiding faces, people and objects, removing or replacing the background, AI Unblur and upscaling, object select and object eraser. The pictures, videos and sound you edit are processed on the device and are never sent to me or to any other server by the app. Projects and exported videos are saved in the app or on your phone where you choose.

Some of these tools need an AI model file that is not built into the app. The first time you use such a tool, the app downloads the model file once (for example from GitHub or Hugging Face, where the open models are published) and keeps it in the app's private storage. Like any download, the server you download from can see your IP address and basic technical details of the request (such as the time and the file asked for); no file, picture, sound or personal information of yours is sent. You can also import a model file yourself instead.

Lock-screen and notification privacy

By default, when music plays from the vault, the lock screen, the music notification and connected Bluetooth devices show "Safease" instead of the song name. You can change this in Settings › Privacy.

3. Data collected by services in the app

Google AdMob (ads)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be worked out;
  • information about ads shown and your interactions with them;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as crash and timing data from the ad software.

This data is sent over an encrypted connection and is processed by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Ads are never based on your vault content or the files you edit, which the ad software cannot access.

Your choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and some US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on the phone). Premium removes ads completely.

Google Play (purchases)

Premium subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. Google processes your payment details; I never receive them. The app receives only the status of your purchase (for example, whether Premium is active) so it can turn ads off. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Google ML Kit (on-device scanning and image tools)

Text recognition, document scanning, barcode scanning, face detection, selfie and subject segmentation (used to find people, faces and objects, and to remove backgrounds) run on your phone using Google ML Kit, with some models provided by Google Play services. The images and video frames are processed on the device and are not sent to me or used by the app for any other purpose. Like other Google Play services components, ML Kit may send Google technical information such as the device model, app version and performance and usage statistics of these features; see ML Kit terms.

Google Firebase Crashlytics (optional crash reports)

After you first unlock the app, it asks once whether to send anonymous crash reports. The box is not ticked unless you tick it, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy › Send crash reports. Until you turn it on, nothing is sent, and any crash information Crashlytics kept on your phone is deleted.

If you turn it on and the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, a technical report is sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics, which I use to find and fix the cause. A report contains:

  • the type of error and where in the app's code it happened (a technical stack trace);
  • a random reference code, the app version and whether Lite mode was on;
  • the last steps before the problem: which parts of the app were opened or closed (internal screen names, never file names or titles), whether the app went to the background, and how long it had been open;
  • roughly how much free storage the phone had (for example "under 500 MB");
  • your phone's model, Android version and a rough memory size (for example "4GB");
  • a random installation identifier created by Crashlytics, which is not linked to your name, account or advertising ID.

A report never contains your files or their content, file or folder names, storage paths, your PIN, your recovery key or anything typed into the app: these are removed from error messages before anything is sent. Reports are processed by Google under the Firebase privacy and security terms and the Google Privacy Policy, and are used only to fix bugs.

Problem reports you send yourself

When something goes wrong, the app can also open your e-mail app with a short report filled in (Send report, or Settings › Report a problem). It contains the same kind of technical details as above and is sent only if you choose to send the e-mail. Your e-mail address is then visible to me, as with any e-mail, and is used only to reply.

4. What I do not do

  • I do not sell your data.
  • I do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, voice or files (except an e-mail you choose to send me).
  • I do not run my own analytics, tracking or crash-reporting servers. Optional crash reports go to Google Firebase Crashlytics only if you turn them on.
  • I do not use your photos, videos, voice or documents to train any AI model.

5. Keeping and deleting data

Your vault lives only on your phone and, if you set it up, in your backup folder. You can delete items in the app at any time.

Uninstalling the app normally deletes the vault and all app data from the phone, including editing projects, preview pictures and downloaded AI model files. On Android 10 and newer, Android may offer a "Keep app data" option when you uninstall. If you tick it, the encrypted vault stays on the phone and comes back if you reinstall Safease; if you leave it unticked, everything is deleted. Backup copies, and files you saved on your phone, stay where you put them until you delete them.

Because I hold no data about you, there is no account to delete. Data collected by Google is kept and deleted under Google's policies.

6. Security

The app uses modern encryption (AES-256-GCM, Argon2id key derivation and Android Keystore) and locks the vault automatically when you leave the app. No method is perfect, so please keep your recovery key somewhere safe and private.

7. Children

Safease is only for adults (18 or older, or the age of adulthood where you live if higher) and is not directed at children. Before a vault is created or restored, the app asks you to confirm your age and accept the terms; it keeps only the date of that confirmation, on your phone, and never sends it anywhere. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been collected through the ads in the app, contact me and I will help.
