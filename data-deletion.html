Deleting your data

Safease (Android app com.indusbyte.safease) by Safease Apps
Updated: 31 October 2026

Your vault never leaves your phone. Safease has no accounts and no servers of its own, so your files, PIN, recovery key, contacts and notes are never on any server to delete. They are only on your phone (and in the backup folder you choose).

Data on your phone

  • Delete items in the app at any time (they go to the Recycle Bin, then delete them for good).
  • Uninstalling Safease deletes the vault and all app data from the phone. If Android offers "Keep app data" when you uninstall, leave it unticked.
  • Backup copies and files you saved on your phone stay where you put them until you delete them.

Data Google's services received

The free version shows Google AdMob ads, and crash reports can be sent through Google Firebase Crashlytics if you turned them on. To delete this data:

  1. Open Safease › Settings › Privacy › Delete data sent from this phone and tap Delete. This turns crash reports off, deletes unsent ones, deletes this installation's Firebase ID (Google then removes the crash reports tied to it from its live and backup systems) and clears your ad-consent answers.
  2. Tap Ads settings and choose Delete advertising ID (or Reset). This removes the link between your phone and the ad data Google holds.
  3. To manage ad data Google keeps, use My Ad Center and Google's privacy controls.

Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days in any case.
